Privacy Policy for SmartMoney

Last updated: September 3, 2026

SmartMoney does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — transactions, accounts, budgets, bills, and receipt photos — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, and is shared only with SmartMoney's own Home Screen widget via a private, on-device App Group container.

SmartMoney does not use the network. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs. We do not have servers, and we cannot see, access, or receive your data in any form.

Siri & Shortcuts

If you use the "Log an expense" Shortcut, the details you speak or type are processed by Apple's on-device Siri/Shortcuts system and passed directly into SmartMoney's local storage — they are not sent to us or to any third party.

Data deletion

Because all data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes all associated data. There is no account to close and no server-side copy to request removal of.

Children's privacy

SmartMoney does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and the new version posted at this same URL.
